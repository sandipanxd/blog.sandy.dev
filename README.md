# blog.sandy.dev

My blog. Somewhere to actually write, not just post links.

I'm the only one who writes and edits posts here, there's no open submission or guest posting. Anyone reading can sign in with email or Google to comment, and I reply to comments myself instead of leaving them hanging. Readers can also like a post if it landed for them.

No fake follower counts, no "join our community" banners, none of that. Just posts, and real comments under them.
